# Content ledger — every claim the film makes, in chapter order

Sources: **DX** = Dixit (1992) *Investment and Hysteresis*, JEP 6(1) 107–132.
**BR** = Bowdler & Radia (2012) *Unconventional monetary policy: the assessment*,
OxREP 28(4) 603–621. **MM** = Martin & Milas (2012) *Quantitative easing: a
sceptical survey*, OxREP 28(4) 750–764. **KIT** = the author's own conjecture,
from `The Frictionless Firm.md` and `How the Argument Was Arrived At.md`, always
marked as his. **FILM** = a teaching device invented for this film, not a claim
about the world.

No other author is named anywhere in the film. Bernanke appears once, only as
"Dixit, quoting Bernanke", because that quotation sits inside DX.

## Deliberate omissions

- Dixit's first non-economic example on p. 127 is omitted entirely, with no
  allusion, summary or on-screen note. Chapter 15 goes straight to the second.
- No author outside DX, BR, MM appears. Bernanke only as "Dixit, quoting
  Bernanke". In particular the working paper's citations of Sharpe & Suarez,
  Penrose, and the other names listed in the brief are excluded, and the
  passages that depend on them are either dropped or restated without them.
